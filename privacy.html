Privacy Policy

EverymanFit Studio · last updated 26 October 2026 (Facebook and TikTok added)

EverymanFit Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the EverymanFit channels to post the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and TikTok account, and to read those accounts' own statistics. It has no other users.

What it accesses

  • Through the YouTube API Services, with the channel owner's permission: the channel's video list, its YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, likes, comments, shares, subscribers), and permission to upload videos to that channel.
  • Through the Facebook Graph API, with the Page owner's permission: the list of Pages the owner manages and permission to publish videos (Reels) to the EverymanFit Page.
  • Through the TikTok Content Posting API and Login Kit, with the account owner's permission: basic profile info (name, avatar) and permission to post videos to the owner's TikTok account.
  • No data about viewers or other people is collected.

How it is used and stored

  • Statistics are used only to improve the channel's next videos.
  • All data, including the login token, stays on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to anyone else.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time at Google account permissions.

Deleting data

Remove the tool's access at any time in each platform's settings (Google account permissions, Facebook Settings → Business integrations, TikTok Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). The owner can delete the locally stored tokens and statistics at any time.
